### 2.3 Affected Groups with social or economic impact

**Social Impact:**

There is some positive and negative social impact after deployment.

**Negative:**

- **Poor social skills:** You could forget how to interact with others if you spend an excessive amount of time playing video games. One of the most crucial things you can do in life is to develop your social skills, but if you spend most of your time in front of a computer, you don't have many opportunities to do so.

- **Social anxiety:** Young individuals find themselves in a never-ending cycle of social anxiety caused due to gaming: they fail to develop interpersonal skills due to the fact they invest a lot of their time playing computer games.

- **Relationship failure:** Addiction to video games may have terrible effects on society. Relationships with family and close friends may suffer significantly as a result. Hiding about the amount of time you spend on video games and ignoring other people to play them excessively can cause disputes, disagreements, and even marriage separation.

**Positive:**

- **Prosocial games promote positive behavior.** To encourage the social aspect of gaming, there are several prosocial games available. In some games, cooperation and voice or text chat communication between players are required. This may promote social interaction and the growth of communication skills.

- **Gaming can be an icebreaker.** Playing prosocial games is just one part of gaming's social component. High school and college students frequently talk about gaming, which may be an excellent icebreaker in direct conversations.

- **Alleviate depression:** People are depressed, and in most cases, depression is the cause of mental breakdown. However, according to several studies, people who play games are less depressed, implying that games can also be used to treat depression.

- **Increased Multitasking Capability:** Games have been shown to improve multitasking abilities. One of the most obvious benefits of playing video games is that you can accomplish more things at one time and conduct your regular duties with greater efficiency.

- **Improved Thinking Skills:** Playing video games improves a variety of thinking capabilities, especially concentration, and visualization abilities. One of the most significant positive effects of video games is the above, which can benefit players in everyday tasks in addition to experts and learners.

**Economic Impact:**

The struggle for the highest video consoles and market share has shifted from simply selling consoles to delivering subscription-based services that provide gamers access to new and previously published games.

The economics of revenue provided by console manufacturers have altered. Companies may originally sell the consoles at a loss to attract users and acquire market share from competitors. The concept seeks to compensate for potential revenue losses by selling games and internet subscriptions.

Developers, publishers, console manufacturers, and distributors are the industry's primary actors. The developers are responsible for designing and programming the games, whereas the marketers oversee manufacturing and marketing.

## 6. IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS

This part will cover game creation setup, which outlines the tools required to create games, as well as game deployment setup, which demonstrates how games are copied to consoles. This section will also define the algorithms and constraints which are necessary to implement the games as well as limitations and restrictions of the system.

### 6.1 Development Setup

The development tools are as follows:

1. **Unity:** -We use unity editor in order to develop our games, unity provides pre-made game engine with its own physics system which helps us to speed up the game making process as we do not have to worry about all the intricate details of our games.
2. **C#/:** - It is the programming language in which we will do our coding.
3. **FL studio:** -Fruity loop studio is used for the installation of music tools as well as adding background music to games.
4. **Asperities:** - This is an image editor which is used for pixel-art and animation.
5. **Raspberry pi:** -This equipment is used in our gaming console to run the games.
6. **Visual studio:** -This is the editor we will be using to code our games with since visual studio is the recommended editor for unity scripts due to its integration and the intelligence feature.
7. **Controller:** -This will help the player to control the player character.
8. **SD card:** -We will use the SD card to flash our android image onto the console.
9. **USB flash drive:** this will help to copy the games into the console.
10. **HDMI wire:** It will help us to display the games on the monitor.
11. **\*Android image\*** we will be using this android image in order to use android on the device.

### 6.2 Deployment setup

After developing our games, we copy them over to our console, for the end user to play. That is the deployment setup of our project, one problem we faced was the development of our game to match the android version of our console, we overcame this issue by building the app to the version supported by the console.

### 6.3 Algorithms

The two main algorithms used by all our games are the:

1. **Game Manager:** This is used to manage all the active states that can be achieved in our game. This includes the play state, pause menu, update state of player and the quit game state.
2. **Scene manager:** This helps us manage the scenes of our game, such as the loading and unloading of the start screen and the game scene.

These both can be improved to cover more aspects (i.e., scenes and states) of our games in the future.

### 6.4 Constraints

#### 6.4.1 Assumptions

Things we assume are true:

1. The user will have an HDMI supported display screen.
2. The user will have a way to copy the games over to the console.

#### 6.4.2 System constraints

The games we need to develop need to be developed by taking the android device's version, when that gets updated, the games would need to be also updated to match the console.

Our console needs a controller to play them, even though mouse and keyboard play is possible, but it is quite messy and takes away from the console gaming experience.

#### 6.4.3 Restrictions

As our console is android base if its memory is full then performance of our game will be affected.

#### 6.4.4 Limitations

Submission of feedback is borderline impossible. Since our games are in 2D, 3D games are not being offered by us, this does mean that they can be played but are outside the scope of our project.
